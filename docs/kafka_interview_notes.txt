Kafka Interview Notes

Apache Kafka is used for event streaming and real-time data pipelines.
Producers write messages to topics. Consumers read messages from topics.

A topic is split into partitions. Partitions allow Kafka to scale and process data in parallel.

A consumer group lets multiple consumers share the work of reading topic partitions.
Each partition is assigned to only one consumer within the same consumer group at a time.

Kafka is often used for streaming ETL, event-driven systems, log processing, and real-time data movement.
